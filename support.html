Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my progress?

Progress saves automatically on your device after sessions. Uninstalling the app clears local progress. On iOS, leaderboard scores and achievements that you submit also live in Apple Game Center.

How do I use Game Center?

Sign into Game Center with your Apple ID in Settings → Game Center. Void Rift can then submit high scores and achievements and show native leaderboards.

How do I unlock ships and weapons?

Earn credits by defeating enemies. Open the Hangar from the main menu to spend credits on hulls, weapons, defenses, ultimates, and permanent upgrades.

The game isn’t loading

Force-quit and reopen the app. Check your network if features that use online services fail. If the issue persists, reinstall the app (this resets local progress).

How do I delete my data?

  • Local game data: uninstall the App from your device.
  • Game Center scores / achievements: managed by Apple via Game Center and your Apple ID privacy tools.
  • Optional web account / leaderboard username: email us with your username and we will delete server-side data within 30 days.
